What we collect

Your name, email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash). For listings: the property details, photos and the WhatsApp number you enter, which are shown publicly. For payments: a payment reference; Paystack handles your card or bank details and we never see them.

Why we use it

To run your account, show and review listings, process payments, send account emails (such as confirming your email or resetting your password) and prevent fraud.

Who sees it

Visitors see your listings and the contact number on them. Our service providers (hosting, Paystack for payments, our email provider) process data for us. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

While your account is open, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal and fraud-prevention reasons.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to [contact email]. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
